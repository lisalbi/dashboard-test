# Put OCEA University on your phone

OCEA University installs straight from the web — nothing to download from an app store.

**Open this link on your phone:** https://www.ocea.org/university/
*(final link may differ — use the one OCEA shares)*

## iPhone / iPad

1. Open the link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with the arrow).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**. The OCEA U icon appears on your home screen.

## Android

1. Open the link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu (top right).
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** (on some phones: **Install app**).
4. Confirm. The OCEA U icon appears on your home screen.

## Good to know

- After your first visit, the app works even without a signal.
- Your quiz scores and certificates save on your phone. Tap **Add your name** on the Dashboard so your name prints on your certificates.
- Passed a quiz? Open the Dashboard, tap **View certificate**, then **Save image** — the certificate saves to your photos, ready to text or email as proof of completion.
- Switching phones? Use **Export progress** on the Dashboard, send yourself the file, and **Import** it on the new phone.
